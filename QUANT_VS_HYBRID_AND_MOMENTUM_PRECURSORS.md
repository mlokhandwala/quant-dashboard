## 2. The 6 Momentum Precursors Engine — Parameters & Mathematical Logic

| Precursor # | Precursor Name | Economic & Market Mechanism | Exact Mathematical Formula & Gate Threshold | Weight |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Precursor 1** | **Capex-to-Operating-Leverage Inflection** (*Gross-Block Commissioning*) | Multi-year plant expansion (`CWIP` $\rightarrow$ `Fixed Assets`) crosses `60%` utilization; fixed depreciation and overhead are absorbed, so incremental revenue drops straight to `EBITDA` and `PAT`. | `FA_Expansion_5Y >= 1.40x` **AND** `DE_Ratio <= 0.35x` | `20 Pts` |
| **Precursor 2** | **Earnings Acceleration (PEAD)** (*Post-Earnings Announcement Drift*) | **Latest Quarterly YoY Profit Growth (`Q_PAT_YoY_Pct`)** accelerates past the company's 5-year historical profit CAGR. Institutional analysts update models slowly over 3–4 quarters, creating a persistent 6–12 month upward drift. | `Q_PAT_YoY_Pct >= 20.0%` **AND** `Q_PAT_YoY_Pct > PAT_CAGR_5Y` | `20 Pts` |
| **Precursor 3** | **Operating Margin (`OPM`) Expansion** (*Unit Pricing-Power Trigger*) | Quarterly Net Profit grows faster than Quarterly Revenue (`Q_PAT_YoY > Q_Sales_YoY`), proving unit pricing power and expanding operating margins right now. | `Q_PAT_YoY_Pct > Q_Sales_YoY_Pct` **AND** `Q_PAT_YoY_Pct >= 15.0%` **AND** `Q_Sales_YoY_Pct >= 10.0%` | `20 Pts` |
| **Precursor 4** | **Stealth Institutional Accumulation** (*Free-Float Supply Squeeze*) | Domestic Mutual Funds (`DIIs`) and Foreign Institutions (`FIIs`) quietly absorb shares over 2–4 consecutive quarters while the stock trades sideways, shrinking the retail free float. | `(DII_Chg_1Y + FII_Chg_1Y) >= +0.50%` **OR** `DII_Chg_1Y >= +1.00%` **OR** `(DII_Chg_QoQ + FII_Chg_QoQ) >= +0.30%` | `15 Pts` |
| **Precursor 5** | **Cash-Flow Velocity Inflection** (*Working-Capital Release*) | Recent 3-Year Operating Cash Conversion (`CFO/PAT`) exceeds the 10-Year average (`>= 80%`), proving customers are paying faster or order-book advances are flowing in before P/E re-rates. | `Cash_Conv_3Y >= Cash_Conv_10Y` **AND** `Cash_Conv_3Y >= 80.0%` | `10 Pts` |
| **Precursor 6** | **Overhead-Supply Clearance & Trend Alignment** (*Stage-2 Base*) | Stock trades above its `50-DMA` and `200-DMA` (or consolidates within `-2%` to `-22%` of its `52-Week High`), meaning zero trapped bagholders waiting to sell at breakeven. | `(CMP >= DMA_50 and CMP >= DMA_200)` **OR** `(-22.0% <= Dist_52W_High <= -2.0%)` | `15 Pts` |
